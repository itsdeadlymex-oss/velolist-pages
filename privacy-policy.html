VeloList Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

VeloList helps people resell items: it drafts eBay listings from photos, and checks what items sell for. This policy explains what information the app handles, who it goes to, and the choices you have. In short: we keep as little as we can, we do not sell your data, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Advertising

The free version shows banner ads from Google AdMob. We request non-personalized ads and do not use the iOS tracking permission. Google may still collect limited device and usage information to deliver and measure ads; see Google's advertising policy. VeloList Premium removes ads.

Subscriptions

VeloList Premium is bought through Apple. Apple handles payment; VeloList never receives your card details. The app only checks whether your subscription is active.

Other services the app uses

  • eBay: listing to your account, and the price and sales information shown on Radar. When you open eBay's sold-price pages, eBay's own privacy policy applies.
  • Apple: Speech recognition (the optional "Speak to Fill In" button) and barcode detection run through Apple's frameworks, and Apple may process dictated audio under its own policy.
  • Google: Firebase, Google Cloud, Gemini and AdMob, as described above.

Server records

Like most online services, our cloud servers keep technical logs of requests (such as the time, the kind of request and the IP address it came from). We use them to run the service, fix problems and limit abuse. Google Cloud keeps these logs for a limited period (by default, about 30 days).

Your choices

  • Use the app as a guest without giving an email address.
  • Delete your account in Settings, under Your Account, then Delete Account. This deletes your sign-in account (email and account ID), clears your saved eBay sign-in from your iPhone, turns off price alerts, and erases the app's saved data on your device.
  • Disconnect eBay at any time from Settings. You can also revoke VeloList's access in your eBay account settings.
  • Photos saved at public links: to have the photos from your listings removed, email us at the address below with the item title or the date you listed it, and we will delete them. Removing a photo that a live eBay listing still shows can make that picture disappear from the listing.
  • Notifications and camera: you can turn each off in iOS Settings.
  • You can ask us for a copy of, or the deletion of, any other information we hold about you by emailing us.

Children

VeloList is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Security

Connections between the app and our servers are encrypted. Your eBay access tokens stay in the iPhone Keychain. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes

If we change what VeloList does with information, we will update this page and its date, and for significant changes we will tell you in the app.
