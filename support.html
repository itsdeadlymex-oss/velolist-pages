Connecting eBay

In Settings, turn on eBay and tap Continue to eBay. eBay's own page opens: it can be blank for a few seconds, then asks for your email or username, and then a password, a one-time code sent by text or email, or Face ID with a saved passkey (so you may not type a password at all). Tap Agree on the last screen and you will land back in VeloList with a green "Linked to eBay" card. VeloList never sees your eBay password.

If it says you need to sign in again, use Different account in Settings. Make sure the eBay account is a seller account with selling set up.

My listing did not post

VeloList waits for eBay's real answer before saying anything is live. If eBay rejects a listing, the message says what to fix (a missing item detail, a category, a photo) and your listing stays exactly as you left it, so you can fix it and tap Try again. Nothing is posted until you see "Live on eBay".

Radar shows "No sales found" or "Too early to tell"

Radar's sell speed comes from eBay's own sold counts on the best-matching listings, not an estimate. "No sales found" means none of those listings has ever sold. "Too early to tell" means the matching listings are brand new. Tap Sold prices to see eBay's completed sales yourself. eBay asks you to sign in the first time to show them.

Subscriptions

VeloList Premium is bought through Apple and renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel it in Settings on your iPhone, under your name, then Subscriptions. To get your purchase back on a new phone, open the upgrade screen in VeloList and tap Restore Purchases. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com, because Apple handles billing.

Deleting your account and data

In Settings, under Your Account, tap Delete Account. This deletes your sign-in account, removes your saved eBay sign-in and turns off alerts on your phone. To have the photos from your listings removed from our cloud storage, email us with the item title or the date you listed it. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Camera, microphone and notifications

The camera is used to photograph items. The microphone and speech recognition are only used if you tap Speak to Fill In. Notifications are only used for price-drop alerts you turn on. You can change any of these in iOS Settings.
