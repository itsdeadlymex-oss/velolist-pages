Frequently Asked Questions

How does VeloList estimate prices?
VeloList uses AI (Google's Gemini) to analyze a photo of your item and estimate a fair resale price, along with a title, description, and category.

Does VeloList automatically post my listing to eBay or Facebook?
VeloList prepares your listing — title, price, description, category, shipping details — so it's ready to go. You review and complete posting on your marketplace of choice.

Is my eBay password stored anywhere?
No. Signing into eBay uses eBay's own secure sign-in page — VeloList never sees your password.

Where is my data stored?
Your listings, drafts, and history are stored locally on your device.
